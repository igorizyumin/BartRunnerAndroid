# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 9, 2026**

BART Runner is an independent, free and open-source third-party Android application developed by Igor Izyumin with contributions from volunteers. It is not owned, operated, or endorsed by Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART).

This Privacy Policy explains what information Igor Izyumin, the developer of BART Runner ("Developer," "we," "us," or "our"), handles in connection with the app. BART Runner is designed to work without an account and without advertising or analytics services.

## Information we collect

### Information stored on your device

BART Runner stores certain app data locally on your device so that the app can remember your choices and provide its features. This information is used by the app on your device and is not uploaded to the Developer as part of the app's normal operation. The information may be removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, subject to Android's device backup and restoration features.

### Information sent when you use the app

To provide schedules, real-time departures, service alerts, and related transit information, BART Runner connects to BART's public data services. The app sends the request needed to retrieve that transit data, such as the selected station or route. BART and the providers operating its services may receive technical information that is ordinarily transmitted with an internet request, such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, and basic network or device information. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies. For more information, see [BART's privacy policy](https://www.bart.gov/siteinfo/privacy).

BART Runner does not request or collect your device's precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, advertising ID, or browsing history.

### Information you choose to send to us

If you use the feedback feature, your email app opens a draft pre-addressed to the Developer. The draft may include the app version, build identifier, Android version, and device model to help diagnose a problem. You decide whether to send the message and what additional information to include. We will use this information to respond to you, investigate the issue, and improve BART Runner.

The Developer does not create user accounts and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, or other contact information unless you voluntarily send it to us, such as in a feedback message.

## How we use information

We use information you provide to us to:

* respond to feedback and support requests; and
* maintain, secure, and improve the app.

The Developer does not use information for targeted advertising, sell personal information, or use personal information for profiling.

## Sharing and third parties

The Developer does not sell or rent personal information. The Developer does not receive or share locally stored app data with third parties as part of the app's normal operation.

The app does connect to BART's public data services, as described above. If you send feedback, your message is delivered through the email provider and email app you choose; those providers may process the message under their own privacy policies. The Developer may also disclose information if required to do so by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of the Developer, users, or others.

## Retention

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you change or remove it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup and restoration features.

If you contact the Developer by email, we retain the message and related correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, provide support, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. The Developer does not maintain a user profile or centralized account database for BART Runner users.

## Security

The Developer uses reasonable measures appropriate to the limited information we handle. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Information sent by email may be handled by your email provider and the recipient's email systems.

## Children

BART Runner is not directed to children under 13, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact the Developer so that we can review and delete it.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of information we maintain about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Because BART Runner does not require an account or maintain a user profile, the information to which these rights may apply will generally be information you voluntarily provide in a feedback message.

To make a privacy request, contact the Developer at **igor.izyumin@gmail.com**. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request, and applicable law may limit your rights or permit us to retain certain information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's practices or applicable laws or requirements change. We will update the effective date above and, when appropriate, provide notice in the app and on the page where this policy is published.
